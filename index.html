Before You Knock

Questions, Answered

Where exactly are the apartments located?+

Across three addresses: Downtown Cairo (walkable to Tahrir and the Egyptian Museum), the St. Regis on the Nile Corniche, and the Hilton Maadi in leafy Maadi. Each listing shows its exact spot and what's within walking distance.

Is breakfast or housekeeping included?+

The St. Regis suites include daily breakfast and butler service. Downtown and Maadi apartments come with weekly housekeeping — daily service is available on request for a small fee.

How does booking directly with you work?+

You book right here. Browse the neighborhoods, peek inside the apartments, hear from past guests — then hit Book Now and you'll see live dates and prices and complete your reservation securely, all on this site. Booking direct skips the OTA commission, so you always get our best available rate.

What's your cancellation policy?+

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in on all direct bookings. For the St. Regis residences during peak season (December–January), the window is 7 days.

Do you offer airport pickups?+

Yes — a meet-and-greet at Cairo International with a private car can be added to any booking. Your host will arrange it in the chat after you reserve.

Are the buildings safe for solo travelers and families?+

All three addresses have 24/7 security and reception. Downtown buildings have keyed elevator access; the St. Regis and Maadi Hilton are fully serviced hotel residences.
